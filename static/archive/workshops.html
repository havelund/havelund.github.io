Conference and Workshop Organization



Current Steering Committee Roles

  • Steering committee member for the RV (Runtime Verification) conference, which I started with Grigore Rosu in 2001 as a workshop. In 2010 it became a conference.

  • Steering committee member for the NFM (NASA Formal Methods) conference.

  • On the advisory board for FME (Formal Methods Europe).

Former Steering Committee Roles

  • Industrial Committee chair for the FME (Formal Methods Europe) conference organization. See the industry committee website.

  • Chair of the steering committee for the RV (Runtime Verification) conference during 2010-2017.
    I co-started this as a workshop with Grigore Rosu in 2001. It became a conference in 2010.

  • Chair of the steering committee for the NFM (NASA Formal methods) conference during 2011-2017.

  • Steering committee member for the ETAPS conference series during 2014-2015.

  • Steering committee member for the SPIN symposium during 2009.
