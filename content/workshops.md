## Conference and Workshop Organization

  
  

## Current Steering Committee Roles

- Steering committee member for the
  [RV](https://runtime-verification.github.io) (Runtime Verification) conference, which I started with Grigore Rosu in 2001
  as a workshop. In 2010 it became a conference.
- Steering committee member for the
  [NFM](http://ti.arc.nasa.gov/nfm/) (NASA Formal Methods) conference.
- On the advisory board for [FME](https://www.fmeurope.org)
  (Formal Methods Europe).

## Former Steering Committee Roles

- Industrial Committee chair for the
  [FME](http://www.fmeurope.org) (Formal Methods Europe) conference organization.
  See the industry committee [website](https://fme-industry.github.io).
- Chair of the steering committee for the
  [RV](https://runtime-verification.github.io/) (Runtime Verification) conference during 2010-2017.  
  I co-started this as a workshop with Grigore Rosu in 2001.
  It became a conference in 2010.
- Chair of the steering committee for the
  [NFM](http://ti.arc.nasa.gov/nfm/) (NASA Formal methods) conference during 2011-2017.
- Steering committee member for the
  [ETAPS](http://www.etaps.org/about/steering-committee) conference series during 2014-2015.
- Steering committee member for the
  [SPIN](http://spinroot.com/spin/Workshops/index.html) symposium during 2009.
