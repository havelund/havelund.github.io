## Authored book

### The RAISE Specification Language

[![Front cover of The RAISE Specification Language](pathname:///img/books/raise-1992.jpg)](https://www.imm.dtu.dk/~aeha/RAISEbooks.html)

**1992 · Prentice Hall · BCS Practitioner Series**

By the RAISE Language Group: Chris George, Peter Haff, Klaus Havelund, Anne E. Haxthausen, Robert Milne, Claus Bendix Nielsen, Søren Prehn, and Kim Ritter Wagner.

A tutorial and reference description of RSL. I wrote the tutorial (Part I) and co-wrote the reference description (Part II) with Anne Haxthausen.
