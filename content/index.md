# Klaus Havelund

[![NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory](/img/jpl-mark.svg)](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/)

![Klaus Havelund](/havelund.jpg)

I am a [Senior Research Scientist](https://scienceandtechnology.jpl.nasa.gov/community/senior-research-scientists) and [JPL Principal](https://scienceandtechnology.jpl.nasa.gov/community/jpl-principals) in the Laboratory for Reliable Software at [NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory](http://www.jpl.nasa.gov), managed by the [California Institute of Technology](http://www.caltech.edu). My research spans runtime verification, model checking, specification languages, and the use of formal methods with large language models.

I am a member of [IFIP WG 1.9/2.15](http://www.ifip.org/bulletin/bulltcs/memtc01.htm), on the editorial board of [FoMaC — Transactions on Foundations for Mastering Change](http://www.fomac.de), and a member of the [ACM](https://www.acm.org).

JPL is a [NASA field center](http://www.nasa.gov/about/sites/index.html) and a [Federally Funded Research and Development Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federally_funded_research_and_development_centers). [More about JPL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_Propulsion_Laboratory).
